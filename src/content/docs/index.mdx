## What is this?

Modern test frameworks like [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) are excellent at clicking buttons and asserting text. But real applications do more than that: they listen to your microphone, render maps you pan with a flick, flash toasts that vanish before your assertion runs, and authenticate with passkeys.

Every recipe here starts from a **use case** — "I need to test voice input", "I need to verify an email was sent" — explains **why it's hard**, and gives you **working code**.

## Got something untestable?

Every recipe on this site starts as a real problem. If you're fighting something that seems untestable, [open a recipe request](https://github.com/untestable-dev/test-automation-recipes/issues/new?template=recipe-request.yml) or ping [@untestable_dev](https://x.com/untestable_dev) on X — if the hard part can be rebuilt as a mock, it can become the next recipe.
